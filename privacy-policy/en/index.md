K.Suzuki (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides this Privacy Policy to explain how we handle user information in the smartphone app “Q-Reversi” (the “App”).

## 1. Information we collect

In providing the App, we may collect and use the information described below. We do not collect information that directly identifies an individual—such as a name, email address, or phone number—unless the user enters it.

### 1.1 Information stored on the device

- Local data needed for the App to work, such as game progress, settings, and tutorial display status
- On-device records such as Time Attack personal bests
- VS mode (vs CPU) match records (for example, wins and losses by difficulty)
- Temporary data used to retry failed submissions (for example, pending Time Attack results)

This information is stored on the device by default and is not sent to our servers, except when ranking or sync features described below are used.

### 1.2 Information sent to and stored on servers (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase (including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions) for rankings and related features. Server-side data is stored in the Firebase Tokyo region (`asia-northeast1`). In that process, the following information may be sent to and stored on the servers:

- **Anonymous identifier (UID)**  
  An identifier issued by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No email address or password registration is required. The same identifier may not be retained after the App is deleted, data is cleared, or the device is changed.
- **Display name (player name / nickname)**  
  A name shown on rankings. Users can set or change it. The server checks uniqueness so that it does not duplicate another user’s name.
- **Time Attack records**  
  Information needed to verify scores and show rankings, such as clears, combos, time bonuses, total score, achievement time, and a per-run identifier
- **VS Quantum AI ranking records**  
  Information needed to show rankings, such as win counts against Quantum AI and achievement times

Ranking lists show display names and the performance information above to other users.

## 2. Purposes of use

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- Providing, maintaining, and improving the App’s game features
- Providing Time Attack and VS Quantum AI rankings
- Preventing and verifying score abuse (including server-side recalculation)
- Responding to inquiries
- Preventing misuse and maintaining security

## 3. Transfers to third parties

### 3.1 External transfers

For the ranking and related features described in Section 1, the App sends information to Firebase (provided by Google LLC). Please also review Google’s privacy policy and the applicable Firebase terms for how that information is handled.

### 3.2 Provision to third parties

We do not provide collected user information to third parties, except in the following cases:

- When the user has consented
- When required by law
- When necessary to protect a person’s life, body, or property, and obtaining the user’s consent is difficult

Through ranking features, display names and performance information may be shown to other users within the App.

### 3.3 Advertising and analytics SDKs

The App does not currently use third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar purposes. If we introduce such SDKs in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide notice.

## 4. Network features

Basic matches, Challenge mode, and similar features run on the device. An internet connection is required when using:

- Time Attack start, result submission, and ranking display
- VS Quantum AI ranking sync and display
- Server-side player name registration and changes
- Deletion of ranking data

Communications performed by the OS or app stores themselves are not collected or managed by us.

## 5. Management of user information

We take reasonable measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of collected information and to otherwise manage it securely. Server-side information is stored on Firebase and protected by access controls such as authentication and security rules.

On-device data may be deleted when the App is uninstalled or device storage is cleared. Because accounts are anonymous, if on-device data is lost, past server-side records may no longer be linkable to the user.

## 6. Disclosure, correction, and deletion

Player names can be changed from ranking screens and similar places in the App.

Ranking-related data (display name, Time Attack records, VS Quantum AI win counts, anonymous identifier, and similar data) can be deleted from **Help & info** in the App. Deletion cannot be undone.

If server-side deletion cannot be completed from the App, contact us using the details in Section 9 and include the player name shown on the ranking. We will respond to the extent technically and operationally possible.

Other on-device data can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing device storage. Because of the nature of anonymous authentication, if records cannot be identified after a device change or similar event, identity verification or complete deletion may be difficult.

## 7. Use by minors

The App may be used regardless of age. Minors should use it with a parent or guardian’s consent where appropriate.
